## Most AI roadmaps stall on infrastructure, not ambition

The gap between an AI roadmap and a shipped AI capability is usually infrastructure readiness:
data access, compute, security, not the model itself.

## Start from what the infrastructure can support

Roadmaps that ship tend to start from a realistic assessment of current infrastructure, then
sequence initiatives around what's actually feasible.

## Sequencing matters more than ambition

A roadmap with fewer, well-sequenced initiatives beats one with many parallel initiatives
competing for the same infrastructure and team capacity.
