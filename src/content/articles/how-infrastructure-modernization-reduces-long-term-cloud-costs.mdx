## Why migration alone doesn't reduce cost

Lifting and shifting workloads to the cloud without re-architecting them tends to preserve the
same inefficiencies that existed on-prem, just billed differently.

## Where the savings actually come from

Real, durable cost reduction comes from modernization: right-sizing compute, adopting managed
services where they make sense, and continuously reviewing usage against spend.

## What this means for planning a migration

Treat modernization as part of the migration plan from day one, not a phase that happens
"eventually" after the move is done.
