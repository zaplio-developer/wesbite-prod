## What an SLA should define clearly

A useful SLA spells out response times, coverage hours, escalation paths and what's explicitly
out of scope, not just uptime targets.

## Questions worth asking before signing

Ask how response time is measured, what counts as an incident versus a request, and how
after-hours coverage actually works in practice.

## Why vague SLAs cause problems later

Ambiguity in an SLA tends to surface during an actual incident, exactly when it's most
expensive to discover.
